# Reserve Me Restaurant Management App Redesign

### Enabling restaurants to join and manage themselves

**Role**
UI/UX Designer

**Product**
Reserve Me — Restaurant Management Platform

**Tools**
Figma

## Overview

Reserve Me is a restaurant discovery and reservation platform connecting diners with restaurants across Kenya.

For the platform to grow, restaurants need to be able to join Reserve Me and manage their presence independently.

I redesigned the restaurant-side experience to move from a **founder-led restaurant registration process to a self-service experience**, allowing restaurant owners to register, onboard, and manage their restaurants themselves.

---

## The Challenge

The existing process relied heavily on the Reserve Me founder to recruit restaurants and register them on the platform.

While this approach could work when onboarding restaurants individually, it created a scalability challenge: restaurant owners could not independently discover the platform and set up their own businesses.

### The opportunity

Create a self-service experience where a restaurant owner can go from:

**Discover Reserve Me → Register → Onboard restaurant → Access dashboard → Manage restaurant**

This would reduce the dependency on manual restaurant registration while giving restaurant owners more ownership of their presence on the platform.

---

# 01 — Restaurant Onboarding

The first part of the redesign focused on creating a dedicated onboarding journey for restaurant owners.

Instead of the founder registering the restaurant on their behalf, owners can now start the process themselves and provide the information needed to establish their restaurant on Reserve Me.

### Registration

Restaurant owners create an account and begin their restaurant registration journey.

### Restaurant Setup

The onboarding flow guides owners through the essential information required to establish their restaurant.

The information is structured into manageable steps rather than asking owners to complete everything at once.

### Completion

Once the onboarding process is complete, the restaurant owner is taken into the management dashboard where they can continue updating and managing their restaurant.

**Onboarding flow:**

**Register → Restaurant Setup → Complete → Dashboard**

# 02 — Restaurant Management Dashboard

Once a restaurant has completed onboarding, the owner moves into the restaurant management dashboard.

The dashboard is designed for **ongoing management**, allowing owners to maintain and update their presence on Reserve Me.

Depending on the area of the platform, restaurant owners can manage things such as:

* Restaurant details
* Menus
* Menu images
* Restaurant images
* Operating information
* Reservations

This creates a clear separation between:

**Joining Reserve Me**

and

**Managing your restaurant on Reserve Me**

## Key Design Decisions

### Self-Service Registration

The experience allows restaurant owners to join Reserve Me without requiring the founder to manually register their business.

### Guided Onboarding

Restaurant setup is structured into a clear flow so owners understand what information they need to provide before accessing the management experience.

### Separate Onboarding & Management

Initial setup is separated from ongoing restaurant management, giving each experience a clear purpose.

### Designed for Scale

Moving restaurant registration into a self-service experience creates a foundation for Reserve Me to onboard more restaurants without relying entirely on manual recruitment and setup.

---

## Outcome

The redesign shifted Reserve Me's restaurant acquisition experience from:

**Founder recruits → Founder registers restaurant**

to:

**Restaurant discovers → Restaurant registers → Restaurant onboards → Restaurant manages**

This created a more scalable way for restaurants to join the platform while giving restaurant owners greater control over their business presence.

The management dashboard then provides the tools they need to continue updating their restaurant and managing their activity on Reserve Me.

---

## Reflection

This project showed me how a seemingly simple onboarding flow can have a much bigger impact on a product's ability to scale.

The challenge wasn't only about improving the interface. It was about changing **who owns the onboarding process** — from the Reserve Me founder to the restaurant itself.

That shift turned restaurant registration from a manual operational process into a self-service product experience.
